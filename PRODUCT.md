# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Notebook is for individual power users who capture, refine, and connect personal knowledge locally. They are writers, researchers, engineers, operators, students, and self-directed professionals who need notes to open quickly, survive for years, and stay easy to search without becoming a project management system.

## Product Purpose

Notebook turns fast capture into durable knowledge. The product should make it quick to create a note, trustworthy to keep it locally, and practical to rediscover it later through folders, tags, backlinks, search, recents, favorites, and portable exports. V1 success means the app feels dependable under daily use: local-first storage, clear recovery paths, stable serialization, and no surprise network dependency.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, exact, durable. The voice should feel like a serious tool that respects the user's attention: concise labels, visible state, explicit data controls, and no marketing ornament inside the working surface.

## Anti-references

Do not make this feel like a landing page, social writing app, team wiki, or decorative AI notebook. Avoid oversized heroes, card-heavy marketing layouts, forced gamification, decorative gradients, vague productivity copy, hidden storage behavior, and fragile editor behavior based on deprecated browser commands.

## Design Principles

- Local trust first: storage, backups, imports, exports, and recovery states are visible product features, not settings afterthoughts.
- Capture stays fast: creating a note, switching notes, and invoking commands must work from the keyboard without navigational friction.
- Knowledge has structure: folders, tags, backlinks, saved searches, recents, favorites, and graph surfaces help users find relationships without forcing a rigid taxonomy.
- The editor is a real document surface: formatting, Markdown shortcuts, task lists, code blocks, tables, links, and serialization must be stable enough for long-lived notes.
- AI is optional and explicit: future AI features should be opt-in, privacy-labeled, and layered on top of strong local foundations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the app shell, editor controls, dialogs, menus, and keyboard flows. The product must support keyboard-only operation, visible focus states, reduced motion preferences, readable contrast, non-color-only state indicators, and responsive mobile capture. Data import/export errors should be written plainly and announced through accessible status regions.
